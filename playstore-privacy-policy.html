Waykeep privacy policy

Applies to the Waykeep Android app (com.waykeep.app). Last updated 13 November 2026.

Waykeep is a sport tracker for Android. It records your activity on the device and does not require an account.

What is stored on your phone

Recorded tracks, track points, markers, photos you attach, personal bests, statistics, settings and the local diagnostics journal are stored in the app's own storage on your device. They are not uploaded to the developer, and the app has no cloud sync.

Network access

The app asks for network access for one purpose: downloading map tiles to draw the map. Tile requests go to the tile provider configured for the map and include the network request metadata any web request carries, such as your IP address and the map area requested. The tile providers apply their own practices; the default basemap is CyclOSM, built on OpenStreetMap data. Tiles you have viewed are cached on the device, and offline mode stops tile requests entirely.

Recording, statistics, exports and the map's cached tiles work without a network connection.

Data you choose to send

Nothing leaves the app unless you start it: exporting a track to GPX, KML or KMZ, sharing a rendered statistics image, sharing a diagnostics journal, or writing exercise data to Health Connect. The destination you pick in Android's share or export dialog receives that data under its own terms. Automatic export, when enabled, writes to a location you select on your device or storage provider.

Health Connect

If you enable Health Connect integration, the app writes recorded exercise sessions, routes and distances to Health Connect. This is optional and off unless you grant permission. Health Connect data is managed by Android; you can review or delete it in the Health Connect settings, and uninstalling Waykeep does not delete data already written to Health Connect.

No tracking or advertising

Waykeep contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking SDKs and no Google Play Services dependency. The developer receives no usage data and has no way to identify you.

Retention and deletion

Recorded data stays on your device until you delete a track in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app's local database. Data written to Health Connect, and files you exported, are outside the app and must be deleted where you created them.

Children

Waykeep is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated here with a new date.

Contact
