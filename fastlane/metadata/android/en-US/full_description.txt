Waykeep is a private, local-first sport tracker for running, walking, cycling, and everyday routes.

Record GPS tracks with live distance, pace, speed, elevation, and optional heart-rate, cadence, and power sensors. Keep every track on your phone; there is no account, no analytics, and no upload of your activity data.

FEATURES
* GPS recording with live stats and a low-battery warning
* Offline-capable in-app map with cached map tiles
* Manual laps and an Intervals tab that follows them
* Voice announcements with per-statistic schedules
* Bluetooth LE heart rate, speed/distance, cadence, and power
* Optional high-heart-rate alert
* Weekly insights: 7-day distance, 4-week average, goal progress, and a consecutive-day streak
* Configurable route color for the map and feed thumbnails
* Follow a GPX route as a planned overlay (no turn-by-turn)
* Optional GPS elevation fallback when no barometer data exists
* Export tracks as KMZ (including photos), KML, or GPX
* Dark and light themes, a home-screen widget, personal bests, and monthly recaps
* No ads, no accounts, no trackers

PRIVACY
Your tracks, journals, and sensor data stay on your device. Network access is used only to fetch map tiles; personal activity data is not uploaded.
